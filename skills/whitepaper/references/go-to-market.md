# Go-to-Market Strategy Reference

## GTM Strategy Frameworks

### Product-Led Growth (PLG)
- **How it works:** Free tier, freemium, or trial → user discovers value → upgrades to paid
- **Best for:** SaaS, B2C, low-touch B2B
- **Key metrics:** Activation rate, time-to-value, conversion rate, viral coefficient
- **Examples:** Slack, Dropbox, Zoom, Calendly
- **Required:** Strong product that delivers value without sales, low-friction onboarding

### Sales-Led Growth
- **How it works:** Outbound sales → demos → negotiation → closed deal
- **Best for:** Enterprise, high-ACV, complex products
- **Key metrics:** Pipeline velocity, win rate, average deal size, sales cycle length
- **Examples:** Salesforce, Oracle, Palantir
- **Required:** Sales team, CRM, lead qualification process, case studies

### Channel-Led Growth
- **How it works:** Partners (resellers, agencies, consultants) sell your product
- **Best for:** Hardware, enterprise infrastructure, international expansion
- **Key metrics:** Partner acquisition, partner productivity, channel conflict
- **Examples:** Cisco, Microsoft, SAP
- **Required:** Partner program, training, certification, margin structure

### Hybrid (PLG + Sales)
- **How it works:** PLG acquires users → sales converts high-value accounts
- **Best for:** SaaS targeting both SMB and enterprise
- **Key metrics:** Self-serve conversion, sales-assisted conversion, upgrade rate
- **Examples:** Atlassian, Canva, Notion
- **Strategy:** "Land and expand" — get users via PLG, sell enterprise via sales

## Pricing Strategy Frameworks

### Value-Based Pricing
- **How it works:** Price based on the value delivered to the customer, not cost
- **Example:** $X/month when the alternative costs $Y/month in manual labor
- **Best for:** Products with clear ROI, enterprise
- **Risk:** Hard to determine value, requires customer research

### Cost-Plus Pricing
- **How it works:** Cost to serve + margin
- **Example:** $10/mo cost + 50% margin = $15/mo
- **Best for:** Commodity products, hardware
- **Risk:** Ignores customer willingness to pay

### Competitor-Based Pricing
- **How it works:** Price relative to competitors (premium, parity, discount)
- **Example:** 20% below competitor price
- **Best for:** Crowded markets, undifferentiated products
- **Risk:** Race to the bottom

### Tiered Pricing
- **How it works:** Multiple tiers with different features/limits
- **Example:** Free ($0), Pro ($29/mo), Business ($99/mo), Enterprise (custom)
- **Best for:** SaaS, products with clear usage tiers
- **Key:** Each tier should justify the price jump with clear value

## Key GTM Questions to Answer

1. **Who is the first customer?** Be specific. "SaaS companies" is too broad. "US-based B2B SaaS companies with 50-200 employees and an existing compliance team" is specific.

2. **How will you reach them?** What channel? What's the message? What's the offer?

3. **What's the sales motion?** Self-serve, inbound, outbound, channel? How does the customer go from "aware" to "paid"?

4. **What's the unit economics?** CAC, LTV, payback period. Do they work at scale?

5. **What's the growth engine?** What's the primary driver of growth? (virality, paid ads, content, sales, partnerships)

6. **What's the 12-month plan?** Specific milestones for customers, revenue, team size, and funding.
